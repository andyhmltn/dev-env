AI Strategy
----
Aim
---
The aim of the AI strategy is two solve two major problems that we have been seeing as a company and as a build team:

1. Output quality => Customers have been complaining of poor output quality in the platform compared to other AI tools. Quality itself is entirely subjective, but there are two main points that cover the quality itself: Factual accurateness and how factual the end answer is. Then _perceived_ quality. So how does the platform feel to use? A simple example of this is that if you present someone with two LLM answers, one with non formatted text and a completely factual answer that responds slowly with a loading state and another with a less factual answer but with proper markdown formatting, paragraphs lists etc, they will tend to prefer the latter
2. Feature parity with other AI tools

How do we address this?
---
1. LLM Operations
	a. Improving model onboarding and onboarding modern models to move away from gpt-4o being the prominent model on the platform
	b. Revisiting whether the LLM service is the right approach and exploring third party open source AI gateway tools
	c. Evaluation as a first class priority in our process
3. Everything becomes a chat behind the scenes, allowing for the user to be able to iterate on the answer, eventually enabling the assistant across all areas of the site
4. MCP & Integrations
5. Skills framework
6. Research assitant v3, removing the loading states and showing tool calling
7. Better tools for the LLM - Todo lists, plan mode & clarifying questions
8. Agent Orchestration - Managing agents in a unified platform across the platform => Sub agents, context management
9. Code execution
11. Improvements to RAG & Search
